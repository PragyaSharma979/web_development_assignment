TechFest 2026

The Biggest College Technology Festival of the Year!

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is coming soon! Our college is organizing an exciting technology festival where students can explore innovation, coding, robotics, artificial intelligence, electronics, and many other emerging technologies.

The festival provides students with an opportunity to showcase their creativity, develop technical skills, participate in competitions, and interact with technology experts. Students from different departments are invited to participate and make this event a memorable experience.

Don't miss the opportunity to learn, compete, innovate, and connect with fellow technology enthusiasts!

Highlights

TechFest 2026 will feature a wide range of activities designed to encourage practical learning and innovation. Participants can take part in coding challenges, robotics competitions, project exhibitions, quizzes, and technology workshops.

Major attractions include a Robotics Challenge, Artificial Intelligence Showcase, Coding Competition, Innovation Expo, Tech Quiz, and Hands-on Electronics Workshop. Winners will receive certificates and exciting prizes.

  • Robotics Competition
  • Programming and Coding Challenge
  • Artificial Intelligence Showcase
  • Innovation and Project Exhibition
  • Technology Quiz
  • Hands-on Workshops

Date / Venue

Event Date: 20 November 2026 to 21 November 2026

Venue: College Main Auditorium and Technology Innovation Center

Event Time: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM

The venue will provide dedicated spaces for competitions, exhibitions, workshops, presentations, and interaction with speakers and participants.

Speakers

TechFest 2026 will feature experienced professionals, technology educators, researchers, and industry experts. Our speakers will share their knowledge about emerging technologies and career opportunities in the technology sector.

Dr. Ananya Mehta will deliver a special talk on Artificial Intelligence and the future of intelligent technologies.

Mr. Rajiv Verma, a technology entrepreneur, will discuss innovation, entrepreneurship, and how students can transform their ideas into useful technology projects.

Registration

Students interested in participating in TechFest 2026 must complete the online registration form before the deadline. Registration is open to students from all departments.

Registration Deadline: 10 November 2026

Seats for workshops and selected competitions are limited, so participants are encouraged to register early. Please provide accurate information while completing the registration form.
