College Launches New Innovation and Technology Center

New facility aims to provide students with advanced learning and research opportunities

BREAKING NEWS: Greenfield College has announced the opening of a new Innovation and Technology Center to support student learning, research, creativity, and technology-based projects.

The new center was inaugurated at Greenfield College campus on 5 September 2026. College officials, teachers, students, and members of the local community attended the opening ceremony and explored the new facilities.

The center is located in the main academic block of the college. It includes modern computer laboratories, collaborative learning spaces, research facilities, and areas where students can work on innovative projects. The facility is expected to benefit students from different academic departments.

College authorities explained that the facility was created because students increasingly need practical experience with modern technology and research tools. The new center will encourage creativity, teamwork, problem-solving, and innovation among students.

According to the college administration, more than 1,000 students are expected to benefit from the center during its first academic year. Regular workshops, seminars, and technology-related activities will also be organized for students.
