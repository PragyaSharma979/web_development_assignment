About CarePlus Clinic

CarePlus Multi-Specialty Clinic is a community-focused healthcare center offering general consultations and specialist services. Our goal is to provide patients with a safe, respectful, and comfortable healthcare experience.

Patients can review our departments, doctor schedules, appointment instructions, and available services before requesting a consultation.

How to Request an Appointment

  1. Review the available departments and services.
  2. Check the doctor schedule.
  3. Choose your preferred appointment date and time.
  4. Complete the appointment request form.
  5. Provide accurate contact information.
  6. Submit the request and wait for confirmation from the clinic.

Please note that submitting an appointment request does not automatically guarantee a confirmed appointment.

Important Patient Notes

Please provide accurate information when requesting an appointment. Accurate contact details help the clinic communicate appointment updates.

If you have an urgent or life-threatening medical situation, seek appropriate emergency medical assistance rather than relying on this appointment request form.

Healthcare Terms

Consultation
A scheduled interaction with a healthcare professional to discuss healthcare concerns and receive professional guidance.
Follow-up
A subsequent consultation to review progress or continue planned healthcare management.
OPD
Outpatient Department where patients receive healthcare services without being admitted to the clinic or hospital.
Appointment
A scheduled time arranged for a patient to meet a healthcare professional.

Patient Support & Instructions

Before requesting an appointment:

  • Choose the appropriate department.
  • Check the specialist's available days.
  • Keep your contact details ready.
  • Describe your reason for the visit briefly and clearly.
  • Do not use the appointment form for emergency care.

Need help? Visit the Contact & Location section or call the clinic during operating hours.
