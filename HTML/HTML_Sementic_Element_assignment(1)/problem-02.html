About GLA University Noida

GLA University offers a student-centered learning environment where students can develop academic knowledge, practical skills, and professional confidence.

Our university provides undergraduate and postgraduate programs in Science, Technology, Management, Commerce, and Arts. Students receive modern learning facilities and opportunities for research, innovation, and extracurricular activities.

Academic Programs

Bachelor of Computer Applications

The BCA program develops programming, database, web development, and software development skills.

Bachelor of Science

The B.Sc. program provides strong foundations in science subjects along with laboratory and practical learning.

Master of Business Administration

The MBA program focuses on management, leadership, finance, marketing, and entrepreneurship.

Eligibility Requirements

Applicants must satisfy the minimum educational qualifications specified for their selected program.

  • Applicants must have completed the required previous qualification.
  • Students must meet the minimum percentage requirement.
  • Some programs may require an entrance examination.
  • Valid academic documents must be submitted during admission.

Important: Eligibility requirements may vary according to the selected program.

Application Process

  1. Choose your preferred academic program.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements.
  3. Complete the online application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Review the application details carefully.
  6. Submit the application before the deadline.
  7. Check the merit list or admission notification.

Admission Terms

Eligibility

Merit List

Scholarship
