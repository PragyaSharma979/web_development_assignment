Sequence Before a Practical Class

  1. Read the practical instructions before entering the laboratory.
  2. Bring the required notebook and study material.
  3. Review the previous practical experiment.
  4. Enter the laboratory on time.
  5. Follow the faculty's instructions and complete the experiment.
  6. Submit the practical record after completion.

Classroom Rules

Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session in which theoretical concepts are explained.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or programming tasks.
Tutorial
A guided academic session focused on solving problems and clearing doubts.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments, attendance, and other activities.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity used to apply theoretical knowledge.
