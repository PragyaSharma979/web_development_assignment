Fest Introduction


    

        Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day inter-college event organized
        by the Student Activities Committee. The fest provides students with
        opportunities to showcase their skills through technical competitions,
        cultural events, sports and creative activities.
    


    

        The event will feature competitions, workshops, performances, exhibitions,
        food stalls and award ceremonies. Students can participate individually
        or as a team depending on the event requirements.
    


    

        "Our fest is not only about winning competitions; it is about discovering
        talent, building friendships and creating memories that last a lifetime."
        

        — Fest Organizing Committee

Important Definitions


    

        
Open Event

        
An event in which eligible students can participate without a
            restriction to a particular course or branch.


        
Team Event

        
A competition in which two or more participants compete together
            as a team.


        
Individual Event

        
A competition in which a single participant competes independently.


        
Registration

        
The process of officially enrolling for a fest event.


        
Coordinator

        
The person responsible for organizing and managing a particular event.


        
Final Round

        
The concluding round in which shortlisted participants compete for
            the final positions and prizes.
