University Admission Handbook

Welcome to the official admission handbook of ABC University. This guide provides information about eligibility, application procedures, required documents, fees, and important admission terms.

Admission Overview

ABC University offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs across various academic disciplines. Applicants should carefully review the eligibility requirements and complete the application process before the deadline.

Mandatory requirement: Applicants must provide accurate information and submit all required documents before completing their application.

Application Deadline: 30 June 2026

Eligibility

  • Applicants must have completed the required qualifying examination.
  • Applicants must meet the minimum academic percentage specified for the program.
  • Mandatory: Valid identification proof must be provided.
  • Program-specific entrance examinations may be required.

Application Process

  1. Visit the official university admission website.
  2. Create a new applicant account.
  3. Complete the online application form.
  4. Upload all required documents.
  5. Pay the applicable application fee.
  6. Review the application carefully and submit it.
  7. Download and keep a copy of the submitted application.
  8. Check the merit list and participate in counselling if selected.

Required Documents

  • Class 10 certificate and mark sheet
  • Class 12 certificate and mark sheet
  • Valid government-issued identity proof
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Transfer certificate
  • Migration certificate, if applicable
  • Caste/category certificate, if applicable
  • Income certificate for scholarship applicants

Important Terms

Eligibility
The academic and other conditions an applicant must satisfy to apply for a particular program.
Merit List
A list of applicants ranked according to their academic performance or admission score.
Counselling
The process through which eligible candidates select or are allotted programs and seats.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students to help cover their educational expenses.
Registration
The formal process of enrolling in the university after receiving admission.
