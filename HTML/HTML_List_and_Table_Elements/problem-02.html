Starters

Main Course

  • Royal Butter Chicken - Tender chicken cooked in a rich tomato, butter and cream gravy. Signature Dish
  • Dal Makhani - Slow-cooked black lentils finished with butter and cream.
  • Vegetable Biryani - Fragrant basmati rice cooked with seasonal vegetables and traditional biryani spices.
  • Palak Paneer - Cottage cheese cooked in a creamy spinach gravy.

Beverages

  • Fresh Lime Soda - Chilled lime drink available sweet or salted.

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

Follow this sequence for the chef's preferred way to enjoy a complete meal:

  1. Begin with Paneer Tikka and a refreshing Fresh Lime Soda.
  2. Enjoy the Royal Butter Chicken with fragrant basmati rice.
  3. Add Dal Makhani and freshly baked Indian bread.
  4. Finish the meal with warm Gulab Jamun.
  5. End with a cup of traditional Masala Chai.
