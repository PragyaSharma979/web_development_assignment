Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

1. Students must attend classes regularly and follow all institutional rules.

2. Students must carry their identity card and required study materials every day.

Attendance

3. Students must maintain at least 75% attendance to be eligible for the final examination.

Note: Students should inform the class teacher in advance if they are unable to attend a class.

Examination

4. Students must bring their examination identity card and necessary stationery.

Late arrival may result in restricted entry to the examination hall.

Assignment Submission

5. Students must submit assignments before the announced deadline.

Assignments submitted after the deadline may receive a penalty or may not be accepted.

Note: Assignments should be neat, properly labelled, and submitted according to the teacher's instructions.

Discipline

6. Students must maintain respectful behaviour toward teachers, staff, and other students.

7. Students must not damage school property or disturb classroom activities.
Repeated violations may lead to disciplinary action.

Important Notice

All students are responsible for reading and following these terms and rules.

Failure to follow the rules may result in warnings, penalties, or other appropriate disciplinary action.
