Understanding Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account is designed for keeping money safely while earning interest. It can be useful for emergency funds and regular savings.

Tip: Check the minimum balance, interest rate and applicable charges.

Current Account

Current accounts are generally designed for frequent transactions and business-related banking needs.

Understanding Bank Cards

Debit Card

A debit card allows you to make payments using money available in your linked bank account.

Credit Card

A credit card provides a line of credit that can be used for eligible purchases. Bills should be paid responsibly and on time.

Always understand interest, fees, payment dates and terms before using credit.

Understanding Loans

Personal Loan

A personal loan provides borrowed funds that are repaid over an agreed period, usually with interest.

Home Loan

A home loan can help eligible borrowers finance the purchase or construction of a home. The borrower generally repays the principal and interest through scheduled payments.

Important Notice

Loan approval, interest rates, charges and eligibility depend on the lender's policies and the applicant's circumstances. Always read the official terms before making a financial decision.

Responsible Personal Finance Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Build an emergency savings fund.
  • Compare interest rates and charges before borrowing.
  • Pay bills and loan instalments on time.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
  • Review bank statements regularly.
  • Protect your banking passwords and OTPs.
  • Do not share confidential banking information with strangers.

Steps for Opening a Bank Account

  1. Choose a suitable type of bank account.
  2. Check eligibility and required documents.
  3. Complete the application form.
  4. Submit the required information for verification.
  5. Complete the applicable verification process.
  6. Read the account terms and charges carefully.
  7. Activate and securely use the account.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage used to calculate interest earned on savings or charged on borrowed money.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment, a regular payment made towards certain loans.
Principal
The original amount of money deposited, invested or borrowed, before applicable interest.
Tenure
The period over which a financial product or loan remains active or is scheduled to be repaid.
