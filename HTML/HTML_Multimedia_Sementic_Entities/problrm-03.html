Destination Overview

Jaipur, popularly known as the Pink City, is the capital of Rajasthan. It is famous for royal palaces, historic forts, colourful markets, traditional food and rich cultural heritage.

Jaipur is an excellent destination for travellers interested in history, architecture, shopping, photography and local experiences.

Jaipur offers experiences for families, students, photographers and history lovers. The city combines traditional Rajasthani culture with modern visitor facilities.

Travel Tips

Best Season

October to March is generally comfortable for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

What to Carry

Carry comfortable footwear, drinking water, sunglasses, sunscreen and a small travel bag.

Local Experiences

Try local cuisine, visit traditional markets and explore Rajasthani handicrafts.
