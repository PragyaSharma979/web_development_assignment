Welcome to the National Tech & Career Expo

The National Tech & Career Expo 2026 is a major technology and career event designed for students, graduates, professionals and technology enthusiasts.

Participants can explore career opportunities, attend workshops, meet industry experts and learn about internships, placements and emerging technologies.

The expo will be held in New Delhi and will bring together organizations from technology, finance, consulting, education, healthcare and other industries.

Date: 15 – 16 November 2026
Time: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM

Preparation Tips

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Research participating companies.
  • Carry your student or professional identification.
  • Prepare a short introduction about yourself.
  • List questions you want to ask recruiters.
  • Choose workshops according to your career goals.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Keep your digital portfolio or professional profile ready.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete online registration.
  2. Receive the registration confirmation.
  3. Check the expo schedule and select sessions.
  4. Visit the registration desk at the venue.
  5. Attend keynote sessions and workshops.
  6. Meet companies and explore opportunities.
  7. Network with speakers, recruiters and other participants.
  8. Collect resources and complete the feedback form.

Important Expo Terms

Career Expo
An event where students and professionals explore career and employment opportunities.
Internship
A temporary learning and work opportunity that provides practical industry experience.
Placement
A recruitment opportunity through which eligible candidates may receive employment offers.
Workshop
An interactive learning session focused on developing practical skills.
Certification
A document or credential that recognizes completion or achievement of a learning program.
Networking
Building professional connections with people, companies and industry experts.
