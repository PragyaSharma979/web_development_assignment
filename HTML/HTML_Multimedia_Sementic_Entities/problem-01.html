Admission Announcement 2026–27

Admissions are now open! Applications are invited from eligible students for undergraduate and postgraduate programs for the academic session 2026–27.

Applicants should carefully read the eligibility requirements and submit the application before the prescribed deadline.

Important: Admission is subject to verification of documents and fulfillment of the university's eligibility criteria.

Admission Steps

  1. Explore the available academic programs.
  2. Check eligibility and admission requirements.
  3. Complete the online application form.
  4. Upload or submit the required documents.
  5. Pay the applicable application fee.
  6. Wait for merit list or admission notification.
  7. Complete enrollment after selection.

Admission Definitions

Eligibility
The academic qualifications and conditions required for admission to a particular program.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students based on merit or specified criteria.
Merit List
A list of applicants arranged according to the applicable selection or merit criteria.
Application Fee
The fee paid by an applicant while submitting an admission application.
